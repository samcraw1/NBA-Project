Zoomed in: how the tables relate

PK = primary key, FK = foreign key. “1” is the parent side of a relationship, “N” is the many side.

Why the middle table exists: a player plays in many games and a game has many players. One row per (game, player) pair resolves that, and it is where the stats live. About 22 rows per game (26,651 rows ÷ 1,230 games for 2025-26).
Why no home team on games: 5 of the 1,230 games have no home team in the data (both teams show “@” in MATCHUP), so a required home_team_id couldn't load them. is_home lives on the stats row instead.
Later: a team_game_stats table at (game_id, team_id) from your roadmap.
